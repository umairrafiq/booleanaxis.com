Common questions

Will I lose my progress if I update the app?

No. Your progress is kept on your device and carries over to new versions. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data does remove it.

The examiner isn't speaking.

The road test uses your device's text-to-speech. Check that your media volume is up and that a text-to-speech engine is installed under Android Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech output.

Does passing the virtual road test mean I'll pass the real one?

No. It's practice. The real test is given by the Illinois Secretary of State — see the Terms of Use.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No. Everything works offline, without signing up.
