Delete your data

Drive Ready by Boolean Axis

Drive Ready doesn't have accounts and doesn't send your information anywhere, so Boolean Axis holds no data about you. The only data is your progress — finished lessons, quiz and test scores and road test results — stored on your own device.

To delete it

  • Keep the app, reset progress: Android Settings → Apps → Drive Ready → Storage → Clear storage.
  • Remove everything: uninstall Drive Ready. Its data is deleted with it.

Either way it happens immediately and can't be undone.
