Drive Ready — Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026

Drive Ready is an app for learning to drive, published by Boolean Axis. This policy explains what the app does with information about you. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

What we collect

Nothing. Drive Ready does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal information, and it does not send information about you or your device to Boolean Axis or to anyone else. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The app remembers your progress — which lessons you've finished, your quiz and practice-test scores, and your virtual road test results — so you can pick up where you left off. This is stored only on your device, inside the app. It is never uploaded. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android's settings, deletes it.

The examiner's voice

During the virtual road test, directions are spoken using your device's built-in text-to-speech service. The app only sends it the words to say, such as "turn left at the next intersection".

Children

Drive Ready is meant for people preparing for a driving permit or licence, including teenagers. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version of the app adds a feature that uses personal information, such as optional accounts, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: support@booleanaxis.com.
